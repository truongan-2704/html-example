# Orthogonal: nearest-work and claim audit

Actual architecture extension: CenterRankConv separates an unrestricted center matrix from rank-limited neighboring spatial interactions, using narrow OHR branches and one pointwise recombination. Nearest works include [low-rank CNN filters](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.06744), [FasterNet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.03667), [LPRNet](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.11853) and ACNet. SVD initialization preserves the center and approximates off-center kernels. Differences and full A/B/C placement ablations are documented in `ORTHOGONAL_RANK_ARCHITECTURE.md`. Originality risk remains **HIGH**; measured compression/CPU speed do not by themselves establish a scientific contribution. The prior execution-only audit below remains valid for those older components.
